# Select Marketing — Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 26, 2026*

Select Marketing is a private publishing tool inside Select, the personal operating system described on this site. It has one user: its owner. It publishes the owner's own posts to the owner's own LinkedIn profile and Facebook Page, then reads how those posts performed.

## What it accesses

- **LinkedIn:** the owner's basic profile (name and member ID) and permission to publish posts as the owner.
- **Facebook:** the Facebook Page the owner manages, with permission to publish posts to it and to read those posts' views and reactions.

It does not access anyone else's account, messages, friends or followers. It collects nothing about the people who see, react to or comment on the posts.

## Where the data lives

The access keys and the record of what was posted (post links, views and reactions) stay on the owner's own computers. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising. There are no cookies, analytics or tracking.
