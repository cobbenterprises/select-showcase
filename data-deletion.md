# Select Marketing — Data Deletion

Select Marketing has one user, its owner, and stores nothing about anyone else, so there is no data about other people to delete. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md) for what it accesses.

To remove its access to a Facebook account or Page, open [Business integrations](https://www.facebook.com/settings/?tab=business_tools) in Facebook's settings, find **Select Marketing** and remove it.

To remove its access to a LinkedIn account, open [Permitted services](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/permitted-services) in LinkedIn's settings, find **Select Marketing** and remove it.

Removing access stops the tool at once. The owner can also delete its stored access keys and post records from their own computers at any time.
